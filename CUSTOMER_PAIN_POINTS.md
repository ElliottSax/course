# Course Hosting Platform Customer Pain Points & Solutions

## Executive Summary

This document provides comprehensive research on customer pain points for both course creators and students using online course hosting platforms. The research is based on user reviews, complaints, platform comparisons, and industry analysis from 2025.

**Key Finding:** 97% of course creators identify sales and marketing as their #1 challenge, while students primarily struggle with engagement, technical issues, and lack of instructor interaction.

## 1. Course Creator Pain Points

### 1.1 Sales & Marketing (TOP #1 ISSUE)

**Problem:**
- **97% of course creators** identify sales and marketing as their #1 challenge
- Hard to get people into the system
- Even harder to nurture leads and convert them to paid students
- Most instructors are not internet marketers and don't know how to generate or nurture leads
- Individual online teachers lack substantial support for holistic marketing campaigns

**Impact:**
- Low course sales despite quality content
- Wasted time and money on ineffective marketing
- High customer acquisition costs

**Current Platform Gaps:**
- Most platforms don't support email automation natively
- Forces use of third-party email automation software
- Integration through Zapier can be daunting
- Limited built-in marketing tools

### 1.2 Platform Integration & Technology Issues

**Problem:**
- Course creators currently hack together **20+ different software products** to address their needs
- Data scattered across multiple platforms
- Complex integrations required
- Platforms for webinars and live video coaching are expensive and hard to integrate
- Most platforms need coding skills to customize

**Impact:**
- Increased costs ($200-500/month in tool subscriptions)
- Data inconsistencies and loss
- Time wasted managing multiple tools
- Technical complexity barrier

### 1.3 Limited Design Flexibility

**Problem:**
- Designs are quite limited and hard-coded
- Can't easily build pages the way creators envision
- Platforms require technical skills for customization
- White-label options often have hidden limitations
- Limited customization options (text, themes, lesson templates)

**Impact:**
- Generic-looking courses that don't stand out
- Inability to match brand identity
- Professional designers frustrated by limitations
- Extra costs for custom development

**Platform Examples:**
- **Teachable**: "Super limited with customization"
- **Pathwright**: "Limited customization options"
- **TalentLMS**: "Limited mobile app branding"
- **Litmos**: "Text editor is limited, doesn't allow much formatting"

### 1.4 Content Creation Challenges

**Problem:**
- Creating engaging videos takes hours of work
- Costs thousands of dollars for professional production
- Requires significant time, effort, and money
- Lack of tools for easy content creation

**Impact:**
- Delayed course launches
- Lower production quality
- High upfront investment
- Burnout from content creation workload

### 1.5 Student Engagement & Retention

**Problem:**
- Low course completion rates (only **10% MOOC completion rate**)
- High dropout rates
- Courses being too long without engagement hooks
- Difficulty keeping students engaged
- Limited tools for interactive content

**Impact:**
- Poor reviews from students who don't finish
- Lower perceived value
- Reduced word-of-mouth marketing
- Lower lifetime value per student

### 1.6 Community Building Limitations

**Problem:**
- Platforms traditionally rely on external tools (Facebook groups)
- Can't create paid communities available only to members
- Limited native community features
- **Kajabi**: Very limited community features despite high price

**Impact:**
- Students feel isolated
- Lower engagement and completion
- Need for additional platforms (Circle, Slack, Discord)
- Fragmented student experience

### 1.7 Lack of Market Research Tools

**Problem:**
- Creators struggle to sell because they skip proper market research
- No built-in tools to validate course ideas
- Difficulty understanding target audience needs

**Impact:**
- Courses that don't sell
- Wasted time creating unwanted content
- Poor product-market fit

## 2. Student/Learner Pain Points

### 2.1 Technical Issues (TOP ISSUE)

**Problem:**
- **79.7% of users** face technical difficulties
- Slow or unstable connections
- Platform doesn't work properly on their devices
- System breakdowns result in frustration and lost progress
- Many students lack high bandwidth or strong internet connections
- Can't catch up with virtual classmates due to connection issues

**Impact:**
- Frequent disruptions in learning flow
- Wasted student progress
- Frustration and course abandonment
- Negative reviews

### 2.2 Platform Functionality & User Experience

**Problem:**
- **More than a third of surveyed students** dissatisfied with platform functionality
- Poor mentorship
- Outdated material
- Bad user experience
- Poor internet connectivity and old technology systems
- Even technically competent learners encounter system issues

**Impact:**
- Lower satisfaction scores
- Higher refund requests
- Negative word of mouth
- Platform abandonment

### 2.3 Lack of Instructor Engagement (MAJOR COMPLAINT)

**Problem:**
- **"Where's my professor?"** - Most frequent complaint
- Students feel online learning is impersonal, isolating, non-interactive
- Feel instructors are not particularly interested in them or the instructional process
- One of the biggest obstacles is **isolation**
- Students feel incredibly lonely and disconnected from peers and instructors
- Lack of adequate personal interaction

**Impact:**
- **Only 10% MOOC completion rate**
- Students feel unsupported
- Lower learning outcomes
- Higher dropout rates

### 2.4 Adaptability & Learning Challenges

**Problem:**
- Switching from traditional classroom to computer-based training is difficult
- Resistance to change prevents adaptation
- Time management is difficult for eLearners
- Online courses require lots of time and intensive work
- Students overwhelmed by self-paced learning

**Impact:**
- Slow progress through courses
- Frustration and anxiety
- Course abandonment
- Poor learning outcomes

### 2.5 Design & Learning Experience Problems

**Problem:**
- Bad learning design (not online learning itself)
- Lack of opportunities for engagement
- Passive video watching without interaction
- No hands-on practice
- Limited feedback mechanisms

**Impact:**
- Boring learning experience
- Poor knowledge retention
- Students feel they didn't get value
- Refund requests

## 3. Platform-Specific Complaints

### 3.1 Kajabi ($89-399/month)

#### Pricing Issues (MAJOR)
- **~20% price increase** (approximately $1,200 annually)
- $71/month entry price is high barrier for new creators
- Forced to pay for advanced features they may never use
- Petition circulating: "Don't leave your loyal users behind"
- **Only 25% of users** would recommend to a friend

#### Customer Service
- Superficial support, failing to delve into issues
- Generic responses
- Basic plan customers don't get 24/7 live chat (despite high price)
- Phone numbers don't work
- No way to communicate effectively

#### Platform Limitations
- Basic plan: only 5 products and 1 community
- Search function incomplete (hard to find specific videos)
- Members can't add multiple pictures to community posts
- Only Pro plan allows multiple websites
- Much functionality missing
- Drag-and-drop sites are poorly optimized with many compromises

#### Email Deliverability (CRITICAL)
- Most emails going to spam
- Negatively impacting businesses
- Customer service not responding to complaints

#### Billing Complaints
- Consumers unhappy with billing practices
- Some charged $400 after canceling free trial
- Difficulties canceling subscriptions

### 3.2 Teachable

#### 2025 Price Increases (MAJOR)
- Price hikes in 2025
- Restructured plans
- Eliminated free options
- Added **7.5% transaction fees** for beginners
- Strains creator budgets

#### Student Limits (NEW 2025)
- Starter: up to 100 students
- Builder: up to 1,000 students
- Unlimited only on Growth and Advanced plans
- Smaller creators hit caps requiring expensive upgrades

#### Limited Mobile Apps
- **iOS app only** - no Android app
- No white-label mobile app options
- Students must manually type myTeachable logins
- Only customization: thumbnail and background picture

#### Missing Community Features
- No native options for live cohort courses
- No livestreaming
- No events feature
- Limited discussion tools
- Missing tools for online course community

#### Customer Service Issues
- Bad customer support
- Unresolved issues
- Slow responses
- Many complaints in past year

#### Migration Challenges
- No real migration help
- Creators must manually rebuild everything
- Courses, content, student data all need manual transfer
- Integrations lost

### 3.3 Thinkific

#### Outdated Interface
- Users frustrated by dated design
- Feels old-fashioned compared to competitors

#### Eliminated Free Plan
- Was once a most praised feature
- As of 2025, no longer offered
- Major drawback for new creators
- Can't test platform without financial commitment
- Behind competing platforms

#### Security Concerns (CRITICAL)
- Paid subscribers can access course content URLs through simple "View HTML" trick
- Content can be easily shared via direct links
- Major issue for content protection
- No adequate DRM

#### App Issues
- Missing native livestreaming
- Android apps plagued by **bad reviews** (2.1 stars App Store, 2.6 Google Play)
- Poor functionality
- Many teachers say apps are "unusable"

#### Customer Service Issues
- Bad customer support
- Billing issues: many charged even after canceling
- Unresolved complaints
- Slow response times

#### Migration Challenges
- No real migration help offered
- Manual rebuild required

### 3.4 Cross-Platform Issues

#### Both Teachable & Thinkific
- Missing real options for cohort courses (live courses)
- Haven't developed live tools despite shift toward live teaching
- Poor customer support across the board
- No migration assistance

## 4. Technical Pain Points

### 4.1 Migration Nightmares

**Problem:**
- Poorly executed migrations lead to **data loss, downtime, frustrated users**
- File incompatibility issues
- Potential loss of critical course data
- Files fail to import
- Missing content after migration
- Action timing out
- Manual file conversion required
- Sometimes need to recreate content from scratch
- Data compatibility issues
- Risk of data corruption
- Accidental data loss

**Impact:**
- **High costs** to rectify data loss
- Business costs from poor data
- Time-consuming recovery
- Student access disruption
- Lost revenue during downtime

**Current Platform Gaps:**
- Most platforms offer zero migration assistance
- No automated migration tools
- Creators manually rebuild everything

### 4.2 Analytics & Reporting Limitations

**Problem:**
- Integration challenges - systems don't talk to each other
- Duplicate data entry across platforms
- Risk of discrepancies
- Traditional teacher-centered tracking is isolating
- Students don't engage with their own progress data
- Many LMS platforms don't provide adequate visibility
- Can't spot trends in real-time

**Impact:**
- Can't identify problems until too late
- Low completion rates (20%) not detected early
- Poor content not identified
- Overly advanced materials not adjusted
- Can't make data-driven decisions

**Needs:**
- Dashboards that update instantly
- Immediate insights from raw data
- Ability to spot performance dips as they happen
- Student-facing progress tracking
- Actionable insights, not just data

### 4.3 Video Hosting & Streaming Issues

**Problem:**
- **Local hosting has playback issues:**
  - Buffering
  - Latency
  - Quality problems
  - Device support issues
- **Bandwidth limitations**: Required bandwidth exceeds available internet
- **No Adaptive Bitrate Streaming (ABR)**: Fixed quality causes buffering
- **CDN issues**: No global distribution = poor experience
- **Format incompatibility**: Codec not supported by viewer's device

**Impact:**
- Students can't watch videos smoothly
- Frustrated learners abandon courses
- Poor reviews about technical quality
- Higher support tickets
- Perceived unprofessionalism

**Solutions Needed:**
- ABR streaming (adjusts quality in real-time based on network)
- Global CDN distribution
- Professional hosting platform
- Multiple format support
- Faster stream startup times

### 4.4 Mobile App Issues

**Problem:**
- **60% of user complaints** relate to usability issues
- Poor user experience causes app failures
- Excessive resource consumption
- Slow load times
- Performance issues
- **80-90% of mobile apps abandoned after single use**
- Apps lose **77% of Daily Active Users** within 3 days
- **Over 85% of online courses** fail to retain students

**Platform Examples:**
- Teachable: iOS only, limited customization
- Thinkific: Android apps rated 2.1-2.6 stars, often "unusable"

**Impact:**
- Students uninstall apps
- Negative reviews
- Lower engagement
- Mobile learners frustrated
- Course abandonment

## 5. Business & Operations Pain Points

### 5.1 Transaction Fees & Hidden Costs

**Problem:**
- **Teachable, Thinkific**: 5-10% transaction fees
- **LearnWorlds Starter**: $5 per sale + payment gateway fees + 5% transaction
- Hidden costs in strict usage limits
- Platforms claiming "low fees" hide costs in transaction charges
- Mandatory add-ons increase costs
- Confusing pricing and refund rules

**Impact:**
- **Creators lose significant revenue** to fees
- Unexpected charges strain budgets
- Difficult to calculate true profitability
- Hidden costs discovered after commitment

**Better Options:**
- **Kajabi, Podia Shaker, Skool**: No transaction fees
- **Udemy**: No hidden fees for one-time purchases

### 5.2 Refund & Payment Processing Issues

**Problem:**
- **Chargebacks**: Customers file with banks instead of contacting support
- Fraudulent chargebacks
- Customers forget purchases
- Students unhappy with courses
- Credit/debit refunds take **7-10 business days**
- Platform-specific policies vary widely
- $10,000 daily refund cap (Teachable)

**Impact:**
- Lost revenue from chargebacks
- Time spent on dispute resolution
- Negative reviews from refund disputes
- Banking fees for chargebacks

### 5.3 Content Piracy & Security

**Problem:**
- **E-learning piracy: $63 billion threat**
- **Password sharing draining up to 30% of revenue**
- **US ranked #1** for digital piracy consumption (13.5B accesses/year)
- Modern tactics:
  - AI-generated deepfakes
  - Encrypted Telegram channels
  - Bypass paywalls
  - Hijack streams
  - Screen recording software
- **Thinkific security flaw**: "View HTML" trick exposes content URLs

**Impact:**
- Massive revenue loss
- Content shared freely online
- Devalued course pricing
- Competitive disadvantage

**Current Limitations:**
- **DRM (Digital Rights Management)**:
  - L3 (software-based) in most web browsers
  - Can't prevent 100% screen capture in Chrome/Firefox
  - OS and browser-level limitations
- **Not technically possible** to prevent all screen capture on desktop/Android

**Protection Needed:**
- Hardware-backed DRM (Google Widevine)
- Dynamic watermarking with user-specific IDs
- AI-powered access controls
- Device, location, and simultaneous stream limits
- Suspicious pattern detection

### 5.4 Customer Support Issues

**Problem:**
- **Customer expectations**: 44% expect <5 minute response time
- **Reality**:
  - **Coursera**: "Unhelpful and often unresponsive"
  - No phone number, only online form
  - Some submit forms 3+ times with no response
  - "A few days" wait times
  - Some never receive responses
  - Can't get in touch with real person
  - **Course Hero**: Only 4% of callers get issues resolved
  - **Kajabi**: Basic plan lacks 24/7 live chat despite $71-399/month cost

**Impact:**
- Frustrated customers
- Unresolved technical issues
- Negative reviews
- Platform abandonment
- Lost sales from poor reputation

### 5.5 Customization & Branding Limitations

**Problem:**
- Hard-coded designs can't be modified
- Limited white-label options
- Can't fully customize:
  - Learning tracks
  - Mobile app branding
  - Layout, navigation, interface
  - Text themes
  - Lesson templates
- **Technical complexity**: Requires significant skills (e.g., Moodle)
- Maintaining brand identity across desktop/mobile difficult
- Vendor lock-in with white-label providers
- Complex, disruptive migration later

**Impact:**
- Generic-looking platforms
- Can't differentiate brand
- Inconsistent brand experience
- Dependency on vendor
- Future migration difficulties

## 6. Solutions & Opportunities

### 6.1 For Course Creators

#### Marketing & Sales Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| 97% struggle with marketing | Built-in email automation, landing page builder, sales funnel templates |
| Poor lead nurturing | Automated drip campaigns, behavior-triggered emails |
| No marketing support | AI-powered marketing copy, SEO optimization, social media scheduling |
| 20+ tools required | All-in-one platform: email, webinar, community, payments |
| Expensive integrations | Native integrations, no Zapier needed |

#### Content Creation Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| Video creation takes hours | AI script generation, teleprompter, video templates |
| Costs thousands | Built-in screen recorder, webcam recorder, basic editing |
| Lack of engagement tools | Interactive video (quizzes, hotspots, chapters) |
| Limited design skills | Drag-and-drop course builder, beautiful templates |

#### Engagement & Community Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| 10% completion rate | Gamification (XP, badges, leaderboards, streaks) |
| Students feel isolated | Built-in community (discussions, groups, DMs) |
| No live course options | Native livestreaming, cohort management, events |
| External tools needed | All features in one platform |

#### Analytics Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| Data scattered | Unified dashboard, single source of truth |
| Can't spot trends | Real-time analytics, AI-powered insights |
| Low completion not detected | Automatic alerts, intervention triggers |
| Students don't engage with progress | Student-facing dashboards, progress sharing |

### 6.2 For Students

#### Technical & UX Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| 79.7% face technical issues | Robust infrastructure, 99.9% uptime SLA |
| Buffering, latency | HLS adaptive streaming, global CDN |
| Platform doesn't work | Cross-browser testing, progressive enhancement |
| System breakdowns lose progress | Auto-save every 30 seconds, resume anywhere |

#### Engagement Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| "Where's my professor?" | Instructor presence indicators, response time SLAs |
| Feel isolated | Study groups, peer matching, community events |
| Lack of interaction | Live Q&A, discussion forums, real-time chat |
| Passive learning | Interactive exercises, code playgrounds, simulations |

#### Mobile Experience Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| 80-90% abandon after one use | Progressive Web App (PWA) - no app store needed |
| Poor performance | Offline mode, video downloads, optimized assets |
| iOS/Android fragmentation | Single PWA works everywhere |
| 77% drop in 3 days | Push notifications, engagement hooks, streaks |

### 6.3 For Platform Operations

#### Migration Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| No migration help | Free migration service, automated tools |
| Data loss risk | Backup system, rollback capability |
| File incompatibility | Universal import (SCORM, CSV, video, docs) |
| Manual rebuild required | One-click import from competitors |

#### Pricing Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| 5-10% transaction fees | 0% transaction fees |
| Hidden costs | Transparent pricing, no surprise fees |
| Student limits | Unlimited students on all plans |
| Expensive upgrades | Pay-as-you-grow, fair pricing |

#### Security Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| $63B piracy threat | DRM + dynamic watermarking |
| 30% revenue lost to sharing | Device limits, login alerts |
| Content easily stolen | AI pattern detection, suspicious activity alerts |
| Screen recording | Hardware-backed DRM (where possible) |

#### Support Solutions
| Pain Point | Solution Opportunity |
|------------|---------------------|
| <5 min response expected | 24/7 live chat on all plans |
| Only 4% get issues resolved | AI chatbot for instant answers + human escalation |
| No phone support | Video call support for technical issues |
| Days to get response | <1 hour response time SLA |

## 8. Competitive Differentiation Opportunities

### Must-Have Features to Beat Competition

1. **Zero Transaction Fees** (vs 5-10% industry standard)
2. **Free Migration Service** (vs no help from competitors)
3. **Native Email Automation** (vs forced third-party integrations)
4. **Built-in Community** (vs external Facebook groups)
5. **Live Course Tools** (vs missing from Teachable/Thinkific)
6. **PWA Mobile Experience** (vs poor native apps)
7. **Real-time Analytics** (vs delayed reporting)
8. **24/7 Chat Support** (vs days-long waits)
9. **Unlimited Students** (vs tiered limits)
10. **Full Customization** (vs hard-coded designs)

### Features That Create WOW Moments

1. **AI Course Assistant** - Helps students 24/7
2. **Auto-Generated Quizzes** - From video content
3. **Gamification System** - XP, badges, leaderboards
4. **Interactive Video** - Hotspots, branching, quizzes
5. **One-Click Competitor Import** - Migrate in minutes
6. **Revenue Rescue** - AI alerts for at-risk students
7. **Smart Drip** - AI-optimized content release
8. **Cohort Automation** - Auto-match study buddies
9. **Progress Sharing** - Social proof for engagement
10. **Content Protection** - DRM + watermarking + AI monitoring
